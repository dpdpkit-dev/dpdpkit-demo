This page runs the real dpdpkit-django app in a small Django project mounted on this site at {{ django_prefix }}/. The frame below is server-rendered by Django: the {{ '{% dpdp_notice %}' }} and {{ '{% dpdp_preferences %}' }} template tags show the notice and the consent form, and two views are guarded by @requires_consent and RequiresConsentMixin. Pick a fictional person, try the buttons (refused), tick "Send you offers", save, and try them again. Data is kept in a temporary database and resets when the site restarts.
