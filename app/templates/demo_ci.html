This page runs both CI tools for real. The lint check calls the same dpdpkit lint that dpdpkit-lint-action runs in a workflow, and the conformance suite runs as a subprocess against two small apps, one that passes and one that fails. Nothing is stored. A pass means the configuration is complete or the API matches the contract; it is not a statement that your notice or retention periods are lawful.
