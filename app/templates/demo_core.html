This page runs dpdpkit-core and dpdpkit-policies directly, with no web framework or database in between. Each request builds a small kit on an in-memory repository, seeded with fictional people, so nothing you do here is kept or shared. The four sections below are the ledger, retention, the rule-to-code map and the sector overlays.
