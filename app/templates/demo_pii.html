This page runs dpdpkit-pii on a small fictional database that was built when the site started: customers, orders, vendors and products, with made-up people, PAN, Aadhaar, GSTIN, IFSC, UPI and mobile numbers. The scanner reads a capped sample of each column through a read-only connection and reports names, entity types and counts, never values. Below, the names in the box stand for what a team has registered (table.column, with * wildcards). Anything the scan found that the registry does not cover is a gap and is listed first. Edit the list and press the button to see the gaps change. 

scan = scan_database(engine, sample=100)
report = build_report(scan, registry=["customers.full_name", "orders.*"])
